# Review context for RDW

Context for `homey app review`. Keep it factual and short.

## What the app is

The RDW (Rijksdienst voor het Wegverkeer) is the Dutch vehicle authority. It publishes the registration data of every Dutch vehicle as open data. The app reads that public API over HTTPS: the user adds a vehicle by entering its license plate, and the app checks it about once a day for the APK (periodic inspection) expiry date, liability insurance and open recalls. It is for Dutch vehicle owners who want reminders and Flows around those dates.

## Choices a reviewer might question

- **Permissions:** none.
- **Cloud connectivity on a local-only app:** the app needs no account or key. It only calls the public RDW open-data API, about once a day per vehicle.
- **Pairing by license plate, no discovery:** a vehicle is not on the network. The license plate is the vehicle's identity at the RDW.
- **Capability values "Ja" and "Nee":** `is_insured` and `open_recall_indicator` show the RDW's own values. Existing Flows compare them, so they stay as they are.
- **Notifications:** the app sends a Homey notification when the APK expires within a month and on the expiry day, at most once per day.
- **Other apps for the same brand:** none in the store.

## Unofficial

This is a community app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the RDW.
